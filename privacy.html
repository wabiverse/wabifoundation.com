Privacy Policy

Kraken and this website do not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

The Kraken app

Kraken for Mac, iPad and iPhone runs entirely on your device and makes no network connections. Scenes and files you open stay where they are and are never uploaded.

Kraken keeps a few things on your device so it works the way you left it: preferences such as the folders you add to the asset library, and caches such as compiled shaders and scripts. None of this leaves your device, and deleting the app removes it.

TestFlight

If you install Kraken for iPhone or iPad through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple collects information about the beta, which can include crash logs, your device model and iOS version, app usage, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. Apple shares this with us as described in its TestFlight terms, and we use it only to find and fix problems in Kraken.

Downloads

The Mac version is downloaded from GitHub. GitHub handles those downloads under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or third party scripts. If you switch between the light and dark theme, that choice is saved in your browser and is never sent anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep server logs such as IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Kraken and this website are not directed at children and collect no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be sent by opening an issue on GitHub.
